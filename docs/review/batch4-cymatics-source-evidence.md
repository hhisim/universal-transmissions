## 2. Authorship boundary inside the 2001 edition

This matters because the file mixes Jenny with four other writers.

- Printed pp.8–9 — **Jeff Volk**, *Publisher's Confession*. Signed
  "Jeff Volk, January, 2001" (PDF p.7). Describes Jenny's Anthroposophy.
- Printed p.10 onward — **John Beaulieu**, *A Commentary on Cymatics*.
  (PDF p.8 onward.) Writes: "I am not seeking scientific precision."

*vibrational medicine*; that framing is **not** Jenny's and is not adopted.

## 3. Claim-by-claim mapping

### 3.1 "Cymatics" as a modern name — SUPPORTED (narrowly)
- **Source/edition:** Jenny, *Cymatics*, 2001 rev. ed., printed p.20 /
  PDF p.18, chapter "Problems of Cymatics".
- **Passage (paraphrased):** Jenny proposes the term *cymatics* for the
  field, deriving it from Greek — *ta kymata*, matters pertaining to waves.
- **Supports:** that Jenny supplied the modern name and the Greek derivation.
- **Does NOT establish:** that an ancient Greek discipline named "cymatics"
  existed. The word is presented as his own coinage.

### 3.2 Experiment / interpretation boundary — SUPPORTED
- **Source/location:** same page, printed p.20 / PDF p.18.
- **Passage (paraphrased):** the work must proceed on strictly empirical and
  phenomenological lines, and all interpretative or analogical thinking is
  out of place.
- **Supports:** the page's experiment-vs-interpretation boundary, and it is
  Jenny's own stated method — not commentary imposed on him.

### 3.3 Chladni plate-and-powder experiment — SUPPORTED
- **Source/location:** printed p.21 / PDF p.19, "Experimental Method".
- **Passage (paraphrased):** Jenny describes Chladni bowing powder-covered
  metal plates, and the powder moving off the antinodes onto the nodal lines.
- **Supports:** the described plate experiment.
- **Does NOT establish:** any healing, therapeutic or medical outcome.

### 3.4 Chladni's publication record — SUPPORTED (APS; primary confirmed)
- **Source/location:** APS News article, 8 July 2017, body paragraph on
  Chladni's output.
- **Passage (paraphrased):** Chladni published results including **11 plates
  and 166 figures** in his **1787** treatise *Entdeckungen über die Theorie
  des Klanges*; his most seminal treatise was **1802's** *Die Akustik*.
- **Independent confirmation:** archive.org record for
  `entdeckungenuber00chla` gives title *Entdeckungen über die Theorie des
  Klanges*, creator Chladni, Ernst Florens Friedrich, 1756-1827, date
  **1787**, publisher Leipzig : Bey Weidmanns Erben und Reich, language ger.
- **Supports:** the 1787/1802 dating and the plate/figure counts as stated.
- **Does NOT establish:** plate-level references inside the 1787 volume; only
  its catalogue identity and date were confirmed.

### 3.5 Hooke, 8 July 1680 — SUPPORTED
- **Source/location:** APS News article, 8 July 2017, paragraph beginning
  "a century earlier, on July 8, 1680".
- **Passage (paraphrased):** on 8 July 1680 Robert Hooke sprinkled sand
  over a solid metal plate, drew a violin bow along its edge to make it
  vibrate, and recorded the patterns as the grains rearranged along the
  vibrational nodes. The article notes there is **no specific mention in
  Chladni's surviving writings**.
- **Supports:** Hooke, the 8 July 1680 date, and the metal-plate / bow /
  nodal-sand description used on the page.
- **Does NOT establish:** that Chladni knew of Hooke. The article says the
  opposite is likely unknowable, so the page must not imply influence.

### 3.6 Leonardo and Galileo — SUPPORTED without dates
- **Source/location:** APS News article, opening section.
- **Passage (paraphrased):** Leonardo da Vinci noted unusual patterns formed
  by particles responding to vibration; Galileo likewise noticed bristle on a
  sounding board moving in some areas but not others.
- **Supports:** naming both as earlier observers.
- **Does NOT establish:** any date. **The previous "around 1630" was
  removed** — neither source gives one, and the 1665 figure could not be
  confirmed from the documents opened. The date is now absent rather than
  guessed.

### 3.7 "Force-field belief" attributed to Jenny — REMOVED, NOT REWORDED
This attribution was **unverified and is not repairable from the accessible
source**, so it was deleted rather than softened.

- **What was searched:** all 135 PDF pages, via `pdftotext`, for
  `force field`, `force-field`, `invisible force`, `unseen force`, `etheric`,
  `subtle body`, `spiritual force`, `dynamic force`.
- **Result:** no occurrence of any force-field formulation. The single
  `dynamic force` hit is unrelated prose about materials being thrust up.
  Anthroposophy appears only in Volk's/Stuten's biography, not as Jenny's
  own claim.
- **Why deletion, not paraphrase:** the 2001 PDF ends at printed p.126 with
  no Volume II text, so a negative claim about what Jenny believed across
  the whole book cannot be supported. Asserting one — in either direction —
  would repeat the error already corrected in this batch with the
  "824 entries" absence claim.
- **Replacement wording is bounded to what was read:** in the portion of his
  text examined here he does not treat the figures as a force field or any
  other force, immediately after his own empirical-only statement (§3.2).

### 3.8 Publication dating — SUPPORTED
- **Source/location:** PDF p.2 title page — Volume I **1967**, Volume II
  **1974**; PDF p.3 — revised edition **2001**.
- **Supports:** the page's 1967 *Kymatik* and 1974 Volume II dating.
- **Does NOT establish:** that the scanned file contains Volume II. It does
  not; the contents page lists it, the body ends before it.

A repo-wide sweep after the page-body fix found the same defects repeated in

  appears - as his belief"** -> removed, same reason.

### 3.10 A test was enforcing the falsehood
`scripts/batch4-cymatics.js` contained an assertion that *required* the
force-field wording to be present (it matched the trailing clause "not a
measurement"). That assertion is what allowed an unverifiable attribution to
survive repeated review passes. It was replaced with guards asserting the
attribution is **absent** from both page and grounding block, that Jenny's
real method statement is present, that 1630 is gone, and that Volume II is
1974. Assertion count rose 142 -> 146.

Lesson recorded: a passing assertion that requires an unverified claim is
worse than no assertion, because it converts a guess into a regression guard.

## 4. Labelling of APS

8 July 2017 — **not** an original experimental publication and not a
peer-reviewed paper. It is a secondary source, used here only for the
1787/1802 publication record and the Hooke date, and labelled as such on the
page.
